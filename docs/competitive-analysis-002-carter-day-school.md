## 4. Where each incumbent is exposed

**Vincent Smith — the closest competitor and the clearest opening.**
Its tuition page states plainly that the school **"does not offer financial aid or scholarships,"** while simultaneously explaining the reimbursement pathway and listing 16 attorneys. That is a coherent strategy — serve families who can front $78,500 and wait — and it structurally excludes every family who cannot. Combined with the finding that **71% of NYC tuition-payment recipients are white against 12.5% of the SWD population**, this is both a market opening and a reform-risk hedge.

**Winston Prep LI — capacity, not scarcity.**
An **80% acceptance rate on rolling admission** is the single most useful competitive datapoint in this analysis. It says Long Island demand at the high end is not supply-constrained. Any thesis that assumes LI families are queuing for seats is contradicted by the incumbent's own admissions posture. Winston also starts at grade 3, leaving grades 1–2 open.

**Stephen Gaynor — right location, wrong access.**
Sits on the densest Carter block in New York State and is correspondingly selective and oversubscribed. Not beatable on location from Long Island; beatable only on access and grade-band availability.

**All incumbents — nobody publishes outcomes.**
Per the Manhattan Institute: these schools "are not required to report student progress, graduation rates, attendance, or postsecondary outcomes," and consequently "NYC DOE cannot evaluate program quality or effectiveness." Every established competitor shares this vulnerability. It is the one dimension where a first-year school can lead immediately, at near-zero cost, and it doubles as insurance against the coming reform.

## 7. Verdict

The competitive set is **small, expensive, geographically concentrated in NYC, and uniformly closed to families who cannot pay up front**. Long Island's two incumbents show available capacity rather than scarcity, which argues against a Nassau-serving-Nassau thesis and for either resiting toward NYC reach or explicitly recruiting NYC families.

The defensible entry position is not "another good LD school." It is **the school that families without $78,500 in August can actually attend, led by someone who can win the hearing, publishing the outcomes nobody else reports.**
