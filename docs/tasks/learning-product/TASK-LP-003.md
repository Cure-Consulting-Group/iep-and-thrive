## Problem and intended outcome

No repository evidence establishes retention, willingness to pay, or learning effectiveness for a standalone product. Design a pilot that can distinguish usability, learning signals, and business demand.

## Implementation scope

1. Recruit a proposed 8–12 family feasibility sample and 3–5 independent practitioners, including participants outside the founder’s tutoring practice; final sample depends on recruitment and research review.
2. Document inclusion/exclusion, assent/consent, accessibility support, withdrawal, adverse experience reporting, adult assistance, and support minutes.
3. Predefine descriptive measures: eligible participants, starts, completed supported and independent tasks, return use, missing data, external paid commitments, and reasons for dropout.
4. Separate feasibility from causal evaluation; propose the next comparison study with qualified evaluation input if feasibility warrants it.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Given** a participant withdraws, **when** the request is processed, **then** collection stops and the agreed deletion/retention policy is followed.

- **Given** the pilot closes, **when** results are reported, **then** denominators, assistance, attrition, and uncertainty are visible, with no unsupported effectiveness claim.

## Validation and evidence

Protocol and consent review, mock participant rehearsal, data dictionary, interview guides, and a blank results template. Pilot execution additionally waits for all G2 safety and reliability blockers.

## Rollout, migration, and recovery

This ticket approves a protocol, not child recruitment or collection. Keep research records access-controlled; publish only separately authorized material.
