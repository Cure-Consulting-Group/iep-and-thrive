# MVP sprint plan

Fourteen two-week sprints start Monday 14 September 2026. Stage 1 repairs the product's truthful
and lawful baseline, Stage 2 builds enough real teaching to reach week eight, and Stage 3 measures
the answer and ships. Dates are calendar dates; points are planning estimates, not delivery
commitments.

Content authoring (EPIC-M4) and audio production (EPIC-M5) start in sprint 2 and run in parallel
with repair work and with each other. They do not wait for Stage 1 repair to finish. Their outputs
are consumed only after the schema, audio catalog, and engine contracts validate them.

## Stage gates and milestones

### Stage 1 gate — Repair, 23 October 2026

The app launches directly into a real item, has no automatic access interruption, contains no
phantom engine entries, has the schema/validator/CI contract, and passes the unconsented-path
network tests available at this stage. The written security and retention policies exist and the
age/gate implementation is reviewable.

### Stage 2 gate — Teach, 12 February 2027

The app has about 120 reachable validated levels, recorded instructional audio, blending and word
building, inferred easy-biased placement, mastery plus spaced review, skippable narration, clean
exit, and versioned local skill evidence. Ticket 041's no-network invariant passes for the default
path. If this gate fails, recruitment and the retention claim do not proceed.

### Stage 3 gate — Measure and ship, 26 March 2027

The consented cohort path is off by default, visible, revocable, code-authenticated, aggregate-only,
write-only, and covered by deny-by-default rules. The measurement plan and 30–50-child recruitment
are complete; golden-file and migration recovery tests pass; App Store submission evidence is
recorded. This is the March 2027 ship milestone.

### May 2027 answer milestone

By the end of May 2027, analyze the consented cohort's first-open and week-eight return counters
against the pre-registered measurement plan. Publish one answer with cohort size, denominator,
missing-data rule, confidence interval, and limitations. Do not substitute engagement proxies or
unsupported mastery claims for the week-eight retention answer.
