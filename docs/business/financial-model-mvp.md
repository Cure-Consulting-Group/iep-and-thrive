# MVP financial model

This is the current MVP planning model. It does not replace or edit the historical [financial model](../financial-model.md); that document describes an abandoned school-and-tutoring business. This model covers the independent reading product and is intentionally explicit about assumptions that have no sourced number.

## Modeling boundary

The cost horizon runs through March 2027: lawful MVP preparation, curriculum depth, cohort measurement, and the first retention decision. The product thesis is independent, phone-native, free-core, and parent-record later. District and school sales are out of scope for years. The model therefore does not assume enterprise contracts, classroom licenses, tutoring revenue, or payment during the MVP study.

## Revenue ladder

The growth timeline supplies the planning ladder: Year 2 approximately $35K, Year 3 approximately $220K, Year 4 approximately $880K, and Year 5 approximately $2.2M. These figures are modeled at 3% free-to-paid conversion and $49/year. They are directional planning figures, not commitments and not evidence that conversion has been observed.

| Year | 3% planning revenue |
| --- | ---: |
| Year 2 | ~$35,000 |
| Year 3 | ~$220,000 |
| Year 4 | ~$880,000 |
| Year 5 | ~$2,200,000 |

The ladder should be understood as a conversion-scaled revenue scenario, not a cash-flow forecast. It excludes refunds, taxes, Apple’s commission, hardship-tier usage, monthly-plan mix, support cost, and paid acquisition because the source documents do not provide those figures. Those omissions are open model work, not hidden positives.

## Conversion sensitivity

Holding the ladder’s implied free-child base constant and scaling only conversion gives the following sensitivity. This is a transparent proportional scenario around the supplied 3% case; it is not a claim about demand.

| Year | 1% conversion | 3% conversion | 5% conversion |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Year 2 | ~$11.7K | ~$35K | ~$58.3K |
| Year 3 | ~$73.3K | ~$220K | ~$366.7K |
| Year 4 | ~$293.3K | ~$880K | ~$1.47M |
| Year 5 | ~$733.3K | ~$2.2M | ~$3.67M |

The conversion assumption is the most fragile assumption in the model. The only public category anchor identified in the research is Prodigy’s “>95% never paid,” on a different mechanic and therefore not a direct forecast for IEP & Thrive. More fundamentally, target families are defined by not having money. The families most likely to convert may be the affluent families the product is explicitly not designed around. The revenue thesis therefore likely needs a philanthropic or grant leg even if the product thesis does not.

## Decision gates and model hygiene

Year 1 is primarily a validated retention curve on a small cohort and an answer on whether teachers recommend without compensation. Revenue becomes a reasonable question around Year 2 only if the Phase 1 retention gate clears. The model must not fund growth assumptions before the product earns evidence of return behavior.

Open owners are: founder for the complete cost budget; educator for authoring capacity; audio producer for recording quote; counsel for legal scope; engineering for hosting and device/support assumptions; study owner for cohort cost; and founder plus finance owner for App Store economics, hardship-tier mix, refund assumptions, and actual-to-plan reporting. Until those inputs exist, every dollar beyond the supplied ladder is an assumption to validate.
